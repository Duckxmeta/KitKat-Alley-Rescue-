Spay / Neuter & TNR Programs

Humanely reducing animal overpopulation in Hohenwald, Lewis County, and Middle Tennessee through community cat trapping, low-cost owner assistance, and hoarding interventions.

Community Cat TNR

Trap-Neuter-Release (TNR) humanely sterilizes, vaccinates, and returns feral/community cats to their outdoor colonies with volunteer caregivers.

Low-Cost Spay / Neuter

Financial assistance for low-income pet owners in Hohenwald and Lewis County to ensure cats and dogs receive essential sterilizations.

Hoarding Interventions

Compassionate rescue assistance, medical triage, and spay/neuter support for high-volume hoarding situations in Middle Tennessee.
