Foster a Rescued Pet

Fostering saves lives. As a volunteer-run rescue without a public shelter facility, foster homes are the backbone of our work in Hohenwald and Lewis County, TN.

We Supply Everything

Kit Kat Alley Rescue provides all food, cat litter, crates, bowls, toys, and routine supplies needed for your foster animal.

Medical Care Covered

All veterinary appointments, vaccinations, parasite treatments, and surgeries are 100% paid for by the rescue.

Flexible Timing

Whether you can foster a kitten litter for 3 weeks or a senior dog until adoption, we match animals to your lifestyle.

Foster & Volunteer Application

Fill out the application below to join our foster network in Middle Tennessee. We will match you with animals in greatest immediate need.
