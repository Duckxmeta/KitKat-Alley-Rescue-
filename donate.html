In-Kind Supply Wishlist

Prefer donating physical supplies? We always welcome supply drops for foster animals in Hohenwald, TN.

Cat & Kitten Supplies

  • Canned wet cat food (Fancy Feast, Purina Pro Plan)
  • KRM / Kitten milk replacement formula
  • Non-clumping & clumping cat litter
  • Flea & parasite preventatives (Cheristin, Revolution)

Dog & Puppy Supplies

  • Canned & dry dog food (Purina, Iams)
  • Puppy training pads & crates
  • Heartworm & flea preventatives
  • Chew toys, collars & heavy-duty leashes
