About Kit Kat Alley Rescue

Veteran-operated, volunteer, no-kill 501(c)(3) organization dedicated to saving cats and dogs in Hohenwald, Lewis County, and surrounding Middle Tennessee counties.

Our Mission & History

Kit Kat Alley Rescue Inc is a veteran-operated, volunteer-run, no-kill 501(c)(3) non-profit organization located in Hohenwald, Lewis County, Tennessee. Volunteer rescue efforts began in 2018, and our official IRS 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status was granted in December 2022.

We serve Hohenwald, Lewis County, and surrounding Middle Tennessee counties by providing medical rehabilitation, foster care placement, adoptions, low-cost spay/neuter assistance, and community cat Trap-Neuter-Release (TNR). Animals in the greatest immediate need come first, regardless of age, breed, or ease of placement.

We are proud to be a Best Friends Animal Society network partner, working alongside national leaders to support no-kill initiatives while operating independently in our local Middle Tennessee community.

Leadership Team

Meet the dedicated individuals guiding Kit Kat Alley Rescue's mission and operations.

President & Founder

Stephanie Pinckley

Founder (2018) • Medical & TNR Specialist

Stephanie founded the volunteer rescue in 2018 and led the organization to official 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status in 2022. She brings extensive medical experience including parasitic disease treatment, basic and intermediate veterinary care skills, feline behavior management, and community trapping/TNR expertise.

Bilingual with a background in digital graphics, Stephanie attended Tennessee Tech, wrote for The Tennessean, holds two science degrees , maintains a BBB grade of A, placed 2nd in SkillsUSA video animation, and served as a SkillsUSA officer at the leadership conference.

Vice President

Dennis Schroader Sr.

Former Lt. Col., U.S. Army Medical Service (24 Years)

Dennis Schroader Sr. joined Kit Kat Alley Rescue in 2018 after connecting with Stephanie. A retired Lieutenant Colonel in the U.S. Army Medical Service with 24 years in uniform, Dennis lives in White House, Tennessee. He has cared for 4–6 rescue cats at a time and is deeply passionate about animal rehabilitation.

Dennis holds a B.S. in psychology from Seattle (1978) and a Master's degree in education and counseling from the University of Puget Sound in Tacoma. He is also the founder of The Price of Freedom Foundation in White House, TN, which honors service members who died in uniform by preserving their life stories.
