Volunteer Opportunities

Join our 100% volunteer team in Hohenwald and Lewis County, TN. Your time and talents directly save the lives of cats and dogs in distress.

Foster Care

Provide temporary shelter, love, and basic care for cats or dogs preparing for adoption in your home.

Animal Transport

Drive animals to spay/neuter veterinary appointments in Hohenwald and surrounding Middle TN clinics.

TNR Trapping Team

Assist with trapping feral and community cat colonies for spay/neuter, vaccination, and return.

Adoption Events

Represent Kit Kat Alley Rescue at local community events, setup booths, and talk to prospective adopters.

Admin & Social Media

Help with digital graphics, email communications, record-keeping, and social media posting.

Board Leadership

Serve on our Board of Directors to guide strategic growth, fundraising, and organizational governance.
